About San

Over 30 years of food, flavor and faithful customers.

Cassandra Williams

The woman behind the flavor

San (Cassandra Williams) has been serving Tyler and the Dallas–Fort Worth area for over 30 years. She specializes in a variety of dishes, including oxtails, smothered pork chops, meatloaf and Salisbury steak.

Her mouth-watering menu has been popularized by satisfied customers who diligently return again and again. San’s the 903 is known not only for the taste of its food, but also for its quality, San’s unique personality and her professionalism.
